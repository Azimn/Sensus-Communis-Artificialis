# Adoption boundary and sequencing, recorded 2026-10-10

This is a research-coordination note, not an authorization for any adapter or another agent's pipeline changes. The standalone Sensus repository may evolve while independent evaluation is underway. All external targets remain untouched.

## Phase 0: through 2026-10-23

No cross-project implementation or dependencies. Existing blinded ablations assess the interval 2026-10-15 to 2026-10-23 and **must not be affected by perception rewiring or viewing-contract changes**. Independent Sensus tests may be developed and audited here, but cannot be installed in or used to alter those measurements. Stable inputs, evaluation conditions and audit provenance take priority over architectural novelty.

## Phase 1: after 2026-10-23 and independent review

Formalize an **audit envelope** at the subject-view boundary without importing Sensus. Every briefing item must carry a source reference and explicit evidence class. Implementation telemetry and raw numeric engine state must not be automatically exposed in the subject view. Engineer telemetry remains separately auditable. Use a reversible, versioned local adapter, regression tests and a rollback path. This is an access and provenance discipline, not a new perceptual binding organ. A date alone does not authorize adoption.

## Phase 2: subject dissent

Provide an explicit, provenance-bearing mismatch record when current readings disagree with stored narrative claims. Preserve both readings and their chronology without silently rewriting the archive, forcibly merging evidential classes or treating a stored narrative as current perception. Evaluate false contradiction rate, detection recall and downstream effect on ossification. Dissent is not proof of a self or consciousness. No implementation is authorized by this note.

## Phase 3: conditional Sensus adoption

An actual multi-channel binding requirement must be demonstrated before any integration proposal. Textual/tool-driven perception alone is not grounds for adoption. Candidate future cases include multisensory village NPCs, external device evidence or embodied signal streams. Require a documented missing capability, versioned adapter, comparators against direct raw inputs, an information-matched decision benchmark, explicit opt-in review and reversible rollout. An architectural diagram is not evidence of need.

## Standing veto and audit requirements

Independent blinded ablations remain sealed. Never share hidden outcome labels with Sensus, its renderer, or its scene. Keep per-constituent evidence classes and source references; never amplify observation through inference or mnemonic recall. Report control-equivalent and negative results as first-class outcomes.

This repository neither implements Synthetic Ipseity, a dissent channel, Daemonium, Witchglass, Pretorius, the village, nor any other project. Dates are planning gates rather than automatic activations.
